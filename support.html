How to play

  • Tap a pocket to lift the group of same-number coins at its front, then tap where it goes: an empty pocket, or one whose front coin matches. A pocket holds ten coins; it takes what fits and the rest stays.
  • Ten coins of one number merge into five of the next. The first time you make a new number, those five open a locked pocket.
  • Deal! drops new piles into up to three empty pockets (four once you have made a 6).
  • The whole game is one run on one tray. Make the coin 12 and the tray is complete.
  • Stand still for a few seconds and a hint blinks.

Questions

I'm out of space. What now?

When no pocket is free and no coins can move, the game says Out of space. Undo takes back your last action and Hammer smashes the front group of a pocket; either usually gets you moving. You can also start a new game.

Why is Deal! grey?

Deal! only drops coins into empty pockets. When none is empty it greys out: merge or combine stacks until a pocket is free.

What does the star coin do?

The star coin, from coin 5 on, is wild. It can go on any stack and counts as the coins of that stack when the pocket reaches ten. To put coins on a stack with a star at the front, they must match the coin under the star.

How do I get rid of a stone?

Merge twice in a pocket right next to it (left, right, in front or behind) and it cracks. A Hammer breaks it at once.

What is the clover on a pocket?

After every Deal one pocket with coins gets a clover. Merge in that pocket before the next Deal and the merge makes six coins instead of five.

Why are some coins showing "?"

From coin 7 on, the back of a mixed pile can come face down. Each coin turns face up when it reaches the front of its pocket.

How do I tell the coins apart without colour?

Every coin has its number on its face and a notch on its rim at its own hour on a clock: coin 3 points right, coin 6 points down, coin 12 points up.

How do I turn the sound off?

Tap the speaker key at the top of the screen. The game remembers the setting.

Boosters and purchases

Coin Cubby has no ads. Free boosters come from playing: 3 Undo and 2 Hammer when you start, one for the first coin 3 to 11 you ever make, one for every three missions, and an Undo and a Hammer for every tray you complete. Free gifts stop at nine of each.

Three optional packs are sold in the App Store: 5 Undo, 3 Hammer, and a Toolbox of 10 Undo and 6 Hammer. They are consumable: added to your stock once and used up as you spend them, so there is nothing to restore. If a purchase was interrupted, open the game again and the App Store delivers it; it is credited once.

For a refund, use Apple's Report a Problem page. Purchases are handled by Apple, so I cannot issue refunds myself.

Your progress

The game saves after every move, on your device only. Progress, best coin and boosters are not synced between devices, and deleting the app deletes them, including boosters you have not used. Nothing about you leaves the phone; see the Privacy Policy.
